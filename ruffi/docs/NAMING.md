# Naming

## Brand brief
Target: 4–7 letters, pronounceable, premium but friendly, not literally “AI travel,” capable of becoming a verb, and ideally obtainable as a .ai domain.

Themes:
- quiet protection
- movement
- anticipation
- companion
- wayfinding
- someone handling details

## Early territories
These are creative candidates, **not trademark- or domain-cleared**:
- Waylo
- Rova
- Voya
- Roami
- Port/porter-derived names
- Via-derived invented names

Avoid generic constructions such as TravelAI, TripAI and TripBot.

## Naming research process
For every finalist:
1. exact .ai availability
2. .com status and collision risk
3. USPTO/trademark search
4. App Store / Google Play
5. GitHub/npm
6. major social handles
7. general web/company collision
8. pronunciation and spelling test
9. international meaning check

Do not buy or publicly commit to a name before clearance.
