Public business proposals

Anyone with a business proposal URL can view its business information, proposed services, findings and sources. These pages do not expire. They are prepared from public business information; they do not reveal private owner answers, prospect contact lists or billing data.

Information you submit

We store your name, email, website, message and any subsequent setup answers in our private AWS database to review your request and provide the agreed service. When you request a free review and agree to follow-up, we use your email to send your review and contact you about the recommendations, implementation and ongoing care. You can ask us to stop at any time. Requesting a review does not authorize website changes or start a paid subscription.

Analytics and email

We use Google Analytics to understand page visits and form interactions. Our custom analytics events exclude form answers, email addresses and private access tokens. Resend handles email delivery when messages are sent. AWS hosts the application and stored submissions.

Access and corrections

Private owner actions require a separate access link. Do not submit passwords, payment card numbers or sensitive customer information in these forms. To correct or request deletion of your information, use the plan form with your earlier reference; we will verify the request before acting.

Retention

We retain submitted information for reviewing requests and delivering and supporting the service. Automatic deletion schedules are not yet configured. You can request deletion; operational backups may retain earlier versions for their retention period.
