Public business proposals

Anyone with a business proposal URL can view its business information, proposed services, findings and sources. These pages do not expire. They are prepared from public business information; they do not reveal private owner answers, prospect contact lists or billing data.

Information you submit

We store your name, email, website, message and any subsequent setup answers in our private AWS database to review your request and provide the agreed service. When you request a free review, we use your email to deliver the review and related updates about that request. A separate optional checkbox lets you opt into Welvia tips, product updates and offers. You can request a review without opting into marketing, and unsubscribe from marketing anytime. We record your choices, the consent wording version, time and source page. To request that we stop contacting you, email Nate. Requesting a review does not authorize website changes or start a paid subscription.

Analytics and email

We use Google Analytics to understand page visits and form interactions. Our custom analytics events exclude form answers, email addresses and private access tokens. Approved campaign and referral labels help us measure where requests came from. We retain the last valid referral within your browser tab using session storage and save those labels with your private review request. Direct navigation within that tab keeps the label; closing the tab ordinarily ends that session. Referral labels do not verify a partner endorsement. We do not send your email address to Google Analytics. Resend handles email delivery when messages are sent. AWS hosts the application and stored submissions.

Access and corrections

Private owner actions require a separate access link. Do not submit passwords, payment card numbers or sensitive customer information in these forms. To correct or request deletion of your information, use the plan form with your earlier reference; we will verify the request before acting.

Retention

We retain submitted information for reviewing requests and delivering and supporting the service. Automatic deletion schedules are not yet configured. You can request deletion; operational backups may retain earlier versions for their retention period.
