The idea behind the mark

Bring the current to the neighborhood.

A new way of finding businesses is taking shape. People can ask an assistant to help them explore a service, compare an option or prepare a request. Welvia exists to help independent businesses take part in that journey, with information that is easier to understand and actions their teams can confidently support.

Current gives that purpose a simple visual form. It suggests movement, connection and continuity. A current carries something forward. Our job is to help bring that movement within reach of the restaurant around the corner, the local service provider and the owner who has a business to run.

The image we want to create is welcoming: a small business joining a useful flow of activity while keeping its own character, judgment and relationship with its customers. Technology should make the next step easier for those people.

Why this direction

A mark built around what Welvia does.

We explored thirteen directions, from monograms and doorways to paths, connections and signals. Current became the selected identity because its three flowing lines connect naturally to Welvia’s purpose. The shape is simple enough to recognize at a glance, and movement is already part of its visual language.

A wave does not need a complicated reveal to come alive. Each line can move slightly, respond to the next and return to a calm position. That gives us an animation that belongs to the mark itself, rather than an effect that could be attached to any logo.

It also gives Welvia room to grow. The shape can sit beside a restaurant proposal, a tradesperson’s inquiry form or an article about agent discovery. It represents a shared approach without tying the company to one industry or one piece of software.

Three currents, one journey

Find. Understand. Act.

The first line represents discovery: making a business’s public information easier to find. A customer’s assistant needs a reliable starting point and a clear path to relevant details.

The second represents understanding: making those details useful. Services, menus, locations, policies and limits should tell a coherent story. The business remains the authority on what it offers.

The third represents action: helping a customer take the next approved step. That might be preparing a complete catering inquiry or requesting a quote. The meaning of that step should be clear, including when staff still need to confirm availability or price.

The lines travel together but remain distinct. That is intentional. Being found, being understood and completing an action are different jobs. Welvia connects them into a customer journey that can be reviewed and improved.

How it moves

A little momentum. Plenty of calm.

Every ten seconds, a gentle movement passes through the three lines. The slight offset gives the mark a sense of one current following another. It then settles, leaving space for the content and the business around it.

That rhythm expresses how we want Welvia to feel: attentive, steady and helpful. Movement should support the experience. It should not compete with a menu, a customer’s question or the next decision an owner needs to make.

On-page marks share that rhythm. Browser-tab icons, saved-device icons and social preview images use the still version so the same shape stays recognizable in small and static contexts. People who prefer reduced motion see a still mark.

A familiar kind of confidence

Warmth belongs in technology.

The rounded ends soften the geometry. The deep green gives the mark definition, while the sage and cream palette keeps it approachable. Together they express a service built to work alongside people and the businesses they care about.

We kept the lowercase Welvia wordmark and let the symbol do a small amount of storytelling. The identity should feel comfortable on an independent business’s screen, whether the owner is reviewing a proposal or checking how an assistant answered a customer’s question.

What Current stands for

Small businesses should have a place in the next flow of commerce. Welvia helps them become easier to discover, easier to understand and ready for a useful next step.

A practical identity

The same vector geometry appears across the website, business pages and journal. A compact version serves as the favicon. The mark can be used in deep green on a light surface or in sage on a dark surface, with enough space around it to keep all three lines legible.

This is the selected Welvia identity, not a claim of certification or a promise that every agent will discover every business. Its role is to express the work we are committed to doing: bringing the currents of new technology closer to small businesses, one useful connection at a time.
